# Frame Direction

The frame should feel like a careful creator's workbench: warm paper, real UI evidence, large readable Chinese type, and motion that clarifies the workflow rather than decorating it. The canvas is fixed at 1920x1080; preview scale must never change line breaks or element positions.

Use one consistent caption band at the bottom, coral accents for keywords, and cocoa UI chrome for the closing beat. Screenshots and recordings are proof objects: they should look tangible, slightly lifted from the background, and large enough to read at video size.
